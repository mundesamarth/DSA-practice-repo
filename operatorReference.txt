| Data structure | Add                     | Remove                         | Check/Find                  | Size      | Peek/Access             |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------- | --------- | ----------------------- |
| **Stack**      | `push(x)`               | `pop()`                        | `includes(x)`               | `.length` | `stack[stack.length-1]` |
| **Queue**      | `push(x)`               | `shift()`                      | `includes(x)`               | `.length` | `queue[0]`              |
| **Set**        | `add(x)`                | `delete(x)`                    | `has(x)`                    | `.size`   | —                       |
| **Map**        | `set(k,v)`              | `delete(k)`                    | `has(k)`                    | `.size`   | `get(k)`                |
| **Array**      | `push(x)`, `unshift(x)` | `pop()`, `shift()`, `splice()` | `includes(x)`, `indexOf(x)` | `.length` | `arr[i]`                |
| **String**     | —                       | —                              | `includes(x)`, `indexOf(x)` | `.length` | `str[i]`                |
